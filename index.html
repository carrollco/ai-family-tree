    About this page. An interactive map of selected people, papers and ideas behind modern AI, from Turing's computing machines to language models that act. It is a selected history, not a complete one: many important researchers and lines of work are missing, and a line marks one documented influence, not full credit or a ranking. Years mark the milestone shown, not birth dates.

    Sources. Each name's panel links its primary sources (the papers themselves, award announcements, the authors' and institutions' own pages). The accompanying README lists every fact-check correction made against the two pages this one was merged from, with sources. Naming a person or organization here implies no endorsement by them.

    Photos. Every photo comes from Wikimedia Commons and is in the public domain or under a Creative Commons license (CC BY or CC BY-SA); each keeps its own license, and each person's panel names the photographer, the license and the Commons file. The photos were cropped and resized for this page. People with no clearly freely licensed photo are shown by their initials.
